PARTNER APPLICATION

Apply to join our network

Choose an application type, provide the required details, and upload your identity, address, and business documents. The optional document text scanner runs on your own computer.

Application details and selected documents are securely submitted for review and stored in private server storage. Only authenticated Master Admins can access uploaded files. Text scanning does not verify document authenticity.

Data privacy notice (DPDP Act, 2023)

  • Documents are collected only to verify your identity and prevent fraudulent access to the portal.
  • Temporary storage only: every uploaded KYC file is encrypted and automatically deleted after 7 days, or as soon as your application is reviewed. Only audit timestamps and masked identifiers (last 4 characters) are kept.
  • Official service activation still requires a formal e-KYC on the regulated partner system. This portal approval does not replace it.
  • Upload a masked Aadhaar or PAN copy (hide all but the last 4 characters). Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

Sub-Admin accounts are not created through partner registration. The Master Admin must first authorize the exact name and mobile number; authorized operators can request access from the Portal login page.
