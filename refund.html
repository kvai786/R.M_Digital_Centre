Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy covers failed, duplicate and cancelled transactions.

1. Failed transactions

If a transaction fails but the amount was debited, it is reversed by the bank or provider according to their own timelines. Raise a support ticket with the transaction reference and we will help you follow it up.

2. Successful transactions

Completed transactions such as cash withdrawals, transfers and bill payments generally cannot be cancelled. A refund is possible only where the provider or bank approves it.

3. Duplicate or wrong payments

Report duplicate payments or payments to a wrong biller as soon as possible with the reference. Recovery depends on the biller and provider.

4. Application and onboarding

If an application is rejected or withdrawn before activation, we will tell you the status. Any fee that applies is described at the time it is charged, and refunds of such fees follow that description.

5. How to request

  1. Sign in and open Support, then choose AEPS failure, Payout issue or Transaction issue.
  2. Add the transaction or application ID.
  3. Track progress on the Track status page.
