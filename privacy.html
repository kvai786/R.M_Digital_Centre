Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we handle it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Data we collect

  • Name, mobile number, address and business details you give when applying or contacting us.
  • Identity and business documents you upload for verification.
  • Transaction references and support tickets.
  • Technical data such as IP address, used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use it

We use your data only to verify your identity, run your account, process and track requests, provide support and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data.

3. Documents

Uploaded documents are stored encrypted, used only for identity verification, and automatically deleted after review or within 7 days. Official activation of regulated services needs e-KYC on the relevant partner's own system.

4. Sharing

We share data with service providers and banking partners only as needed to deliver the service you request, or when the law requires it.

5. Security

We use hashed passwords, two-factor authentication for staff, secure cookies, request validation and rate limiting. No system is completely risk free, so please protect your credentials.

6. AI assistant

Chats with the assistant may be kept to improve service quality and are limited to service questions. Do not enter passwords, OTPs or full account numbers in chat.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting support. Some records may be kept where the law requires.

8. Contact

Use the Support section of the portal or the helpline shown there.
